# Harness logo attribution

The monochrome SVGs in this folder are bundled locally so the desktop app
does not depend on a network request to render a harness card. They are
rendered through a CSS mask and inherit `currentColor` from the UI.

- `aider.svg`: derived from the official Aider brain mark in
  [Aider-AI/aider](https://github.com/Aider-AI/aider/blob/main/aider/website/assets/icons/brain.svg),
  Apache-2.0.
- `continue.svg`: derived from the Continue signet in
  [continuedev/continue](https://github.com/continuedev/continue/blob/main/docs/logo/dark.svg),
  Apache-2.0.
- `reasonix.svg`: derived from the Reasonix favicon at
  [reasonix.io/favicon.svg](https://reasonix.io/favicon.svg), MIT.

The remaining marks come from
[`@lobehub/icons-static-svg`](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons),
version 1.94.0, MIT. The product names and marks remain the property of
their respective owners; this attribution does not imply endorsement.
